How it differs

Memory ≠ weights.

Update the store to correct a fact. No retraining, no drift, no re-learn cost.

Policy ≠ runtime.

Guild rules live in the Guild System. Resileos runs state — it doesn't adjudicate.

Credits ≠ cash.

Internal tool units never convert to Merit or fiat inside the product.

Proof, not adjectives

The capacity and honesty gates are published as artifacts in the repository, not as marketing claims: store-hit rate and invent-rate on a fixed probe corpus, plus restart-durability results. The eval harness is in scripts/ops/run_ops_metrics.py.

FAQ

Is this an LLM?

It's a memory and workspace system. You can attach a model as a mouth over retrieved facts. The store is the source of truth.

Can I self-host?

Yes — the repo is open (Apache-2.0). Paid plans are for managed hosting, seats, and operations.

What about the Guild System?

Policy and Compact live in a separate repo. Resileos is optional runtime infrastructure.

Do you sell governance tokens?

No. Merit is non-purchasable. Credits are internal and non-convertible in-product.

What happens when I hit my quota?

The API returns 429 with a clear message and an X-Usage-Quota header. No silent overages.

Do you host my video and images?

No. The world registry stores the path or URL and the facts around it. Generation, storage, and streaming stay in your tooling.
