Pricing questions

Do I need Docker to try it?

No. Open /chat. Signed-out use is a short per-network daily trial. Sign in with your email to save conversations.

How do free keys work?

Sign in and create the free key on Account. Direct API use can reveal that key once after another email confirmation. scripts/issue_free_key.py is the self-hosted path.

What is Founding Builder?

$19/mo, locked for 12 months, capped at 50. Same Builder seat shape: 3 keys and 100,000 requests a month. It does not auto-renew at the founding rate.
