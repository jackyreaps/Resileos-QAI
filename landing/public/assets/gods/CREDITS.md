# Image credits

The public page for this note is [/credits](/credits).

The landing page feathers each portrait with a CSS mask and a shared
edge vignette so the crops blend into the page ground (`#06080e`). The
files in this folder are AI-generated. In November 2026 the named figures
were expanded from the earlier portraits: bronze, marble catching metal,
gold accents, and restrained crystal traces. Not one chrome coating, and
not a flat museum cast. Floating glyphs, watermarks, and voxel dust were
removed. The mask is presentation, not a new source.

Short loops in `../motion/` are a light sweep across the Zeus and Titanos
stills. They are site craft. They are not videos from the engine. A later
hero or patron clip can be attached with `data-video-src` on the figure.
Nothing is shipped until that file exists.

## Site portraits (AI-generated)

Every portrait the landing page serves as a `*.webp` file in this folder was
generated for Resileos in November 2026 and then expanded from the earlier
named figures. They sit on a deep dark ground. No names, captions,
or other readable words were added inside the images. A thorn-crowned bust
is not used, and is not labeled as Zeus or any other Greek figure.

Where a public-domain or CC0 museum photograph existed, that photograph was
used as an edit reference and is credited below. It is not the file shown on
the page. A copy, when kept in the repo, lives in `cc0/` and is excluded from
the public deploy.

## Hades — Bust of Serapis

No public-domain or CC0 photograph of an ancient sculpture titled simply “Hades”
was available at sufficient quality. This bust is the standard classical type for
Serapis, the Greco-Egyptian syncretism of Osiris with Hades, Pluto, and Zeus.

- **Title:** Bust of Serapis
- **Museum:** Vatican Museums, Museo Pio-Clementino, inv. 689
- **Description:** Marble, Roman copy after a Greek original from the 4th century BCE, from the Serapeum of Alexandria (copy of Bryaxis)
- **Photograph:** Jastrow (2003), released into the public domain
- **Wikimedia file:** [File:Serapis Pio-Clementino Inv689 n2.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Serapis_Pio-Clementino_Inv689_n2.jpg)
- **Source image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c4/Serapis_Pio-Clementino_Inv689_n2.jpg
- **License:** Public domain
- **Used for:** “Hades, Memory” (facts with time, Studio)
