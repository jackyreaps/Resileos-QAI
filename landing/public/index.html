Titanos, Engine

The figurehead

Titanos is the engine over the deterministic residual core. It exposes ask, ask_list, and chain. Those calls return ACCEPTED, ABSTAINED, or CONFLICT.

Order, Structure

How the gods are structured

Not a circle of equals. Titanos sits at the top as the engine. Zeus routing stands over the residual core, which is the deterministic leaf and the source of truth. Kin stand as side-interfaces to that core. Demi-gods are summoned for one check and then dismissed. Legions serve under one Kin.

Architecture

Legions under Kin BETA

Merged on main at ee9199ad. This layer is in beta. Kin are role classes whose constraints do not move. A legion is one bounded tool registered on that Kin via register_legion in src/resileos/kin/legions.py. It inherits the Kin's risk_classify gate, evidence_attach, and budget check. Callers reach it only through that Kin's catalogue; there is no global registry. A legion cannot take the residual core, bypass Zeus routing, cross the Hades checkpoint boundary, or turn the verified-cycle mouth on. Example legions for Ollama, JSONL trace, and lesson notes are optional and off by default: register_example_legions registers nothing unless enable=True.

BETA

Hades, Lessons

Facts with time VERIFIED BEHAVIOR

Facts can carry a valid-from and valid-to window (half-open). Ask as of a date, and the answer is the fact that was valid then.

VERIFIED

As-of questions

Athens capital_of as of 1800 returns Ottoman; today returns Greece.

VERIFIED

As-of through chains

The date carries through every step of a multi-hop /chain, so a historical lookup stays on that date instead of silently switching to today.

VERIFIED

Conflicts flagged

When two facts with explicit, overlapping date ranges disagree on the same date, ask() returns CONFLICT instead of picking one.

VERIFIED

Real date parsing

Years before 1970 and ancient years such as 476 parse as dates, not as text. SPARQL queries accept AS OF.

Specialist checks (Demi-gods) VERIFIED BEHAVIOR

Load-on-demand reviewers. They are deterministic checks plus persona text, not a language model. They cannot learn, own the core, or summon each other, and they are dismissed after use.

VERIFIED

Themis

Certifies a result only when the residual core is consistent, Apollo reports success, and the Hades lesson says verified. Missing evidence fails the check.

VERIFIED

Momus

A truly blind reviewer: control history and author notes are stripped first. It lists defects against the stated spec. Missing inputs return need_more, which is not approval.

VERIFIED

Hephaestus

Checks residual consistency, that no scars are left, and the budget guard. A supplied Hermes budget must stay at or under its max items.

VERIFIED

Nike

Passes or fails strictly against explicit success criteria. With no criteria, it fails.

VERIFIED

Atlas

Checks declared loads against the real capacity functions: the Hopfield limit, the mask-stack layer ceiling, the prime tower's usable levels, primorial count, encoder buffer, and evidence length. An undeclared load is not invented.

Iris, Senses

Senses and speech VERIFIED BEHAVIOR

VERIFIED

Grounded speech & summaries

Turns stored rows into template sentences marked GROUNDED. Empty rows return “Nothing stored on that.” This is structured wording, not open-ended prose.

VERIFIED

Script detection

Classifies characters as latin, non-latin, or mixed. A script guess, not identification of a spoken language.

VERIFIED

Image signal stats

Reports mean brightness and edge density from an image array, with a scaffold address. No object or scene recognition.

VERIFIED

Audio signal stats

Reports waveform energy, zero-crossing rate, and spectral centroid. No speech recognition or sound identification.

VERIFIED

Video signal stats

Reports frame count, brightness, and mean absolute frame difference. No action or scene recognition.

VERIFIED

Text signal stats

Counts characters and whitespace-separated tokens. Perception only, not translation or comprehension.

VERIFIED

Perception → Psyche

Hands image, audio, video, or text diagnostics into a mind-state container; lists phase, belt, frames, and drive values. These are state fields, not evidence of awareness.

VERIFIED

Grounded fact cycle

Looks up, stores, and speaks a fact in a caller-provided dictionary. Studio retains that dictionary in session state.

VERIFIED

Credit ledger

Earns and spends credits in process memory and refuses transfers. Not a durable balance. The HTTP free-use count is still client-supplied.

VERIFIED

Workspace tools

Evaluates expressions, edits workspace files, checks Python syntax, and downloads a URL. Workspace-scoped tools, not a general security sandbox.

FAQ

What does Resileos do?

It remembers exact facts you teach it, derives from those facts, and abstains when it does not know. Capability grows as your hubs fill.

Can it answer as of a date?

Yes. Facts can carry a valid-from and valid-to window. Asking Athens capital_of as of 1800 returned Ottoman; today returned Greece. When overlapping dated facts disagree, the answer is CONFLICT.

Does it generate images or video from a prompt?

No. The residual engine compresses images and video frames you already have, with measured round-trip error. Generating new pictures from a prompt is in research, not shipped.

Do I need Docker to try it?

No. Visitors use Studio + a free key. Docker is for developers self-hosting (repo HOSTING docs).

How do free keys work?

This site’s form calls the billing free-key API, or run scripts/issue_free_key.py and put the hash in the API key file.
