Patrons

Roles with edges. Not a circle of equals.

Titanos is the engine. Zeus routes. The residual core is the source of truth. Kin are standing side-interfaces, including Aphrodite and Eat. Psyche is a state container, not a Kin. The Moirai sit under Hermes. Demi-gods are summoned for one check and then dismissed. Hephaestus is Forge patronage, not a Kin. Eat stays Eat.

Architecture

Legions under Kin BETA

Merged on main at ee9199ad. Kin are role classes whose constraints do not move. A legion is one bounded tool registered on that Kin via register_legion. It inherits the Kin's risk_classify gate, evidence_attach, and budget check. Callers reach it only through that Kin's catalogue. There is no global registry. A legion cannot take the residual core, bypass Zeus routing, cross the Hades checkpoint boundary, or turn the verified-cycle mouth on. Example legions for Ollama, JSONL trace, and lesson notes are optional and off by default: register_example_legions registers nothing unless enable=True.

BETA

Iris

Sensors, log readers, and capacity probes. They cannot write residual state. Signal detail is on How it works.

BETA

Metis

Decision routers, risk classifiers, and guide loaders. Output is policy only. They do not execute.

BETA

Hermes

Paths and dispatch. Navigators and separated-cycle reviewers see the artefact and the lesson, not full history.

BETA

Hades

Lessons and checkpoints. Save and restore do not carry history or scars into a fresh packet. Hades is not Studio.

BETA

Apollo

Proof. Only Apollo can mark verified, and only when verify_on_stop passes.

BETA

Aphrodite

Preferences, care weights, and a persona. Chat lines I like X and I don't like X update the preference map. Aphrodite does not write residual facts. Legions: Calliope drafts the next fragment, Ligeia suggests only, Echo records a lesson through Hades.

BETA

Eat

Structured ingest and perception helpers. perception is an alias of eat. They cannot write the residual store before the risk and budget gates. The public name stays Eat.

BETA

Psyche

A state container on the Hades checkpoint: phase, belt, residual bin, persona, mood. It is not a Kin seat and not evidence of awareness.

Hermes, Coordination

Moirai BETA

Clotho, Lachesis, Atropos, and Harmonia are not a separate Kin. They sit under Hermes. Shared work is kept only after residual consistency and verify_on_stop. These portraits are invented for the site. No sculpture reference.

BETA

Clotho

Registers a process group or cancel callback for work Hermes owns. A Forge worker is a Clotho group.

BETA

Lachesis

Expands another legion or a deeper cycle only when the expected gain exceeds the budget cost.

BETA

Atropos

On stop, sends the session interrupt and terminates the owned group. stop_requested is not fully_stopped. Hades checkpoints a stopped owner only after the group is fully stopped and residual consistency holds.

BETA

Harmonia

Shares a residual step when residual-hash or control-packet prefix overlap is at least 0.8. Accepted only after residual consistency and verify_on_stop.

Aphrodite, Legions

Calliope, Ligeia, Echo

Named legions on Aphrodite. They do not write residual facts. Example legions such as ollama_local, jsonl_trace, and lesson_notes stay off unless register_example_legions is called with enable=True. Studio tools stay under Hermes and Eat: plan/test/debug, graphify, and forge video.

BETA

Calliope

Drafts the next fragment from the preference map and a residual hash. Does not write residual facts.

BETA

Ligeia

Suggestion only. Does not write residual facts.

BETA

Echo

Lesson only, through Hades. Does not write residual facts.

Specialist checks VERIFIED BEHAVIOR

Load-on-demand reviewers. Deterministic checks plus persona text, not a language model. They cannot learn, own the core, or summon each other, and they are dismissed after use.

VERIFIED

Themis

Certifies a result only when the residual core is consistent, Apollo reports success, and the Hades lesson says verified. Missing evidence fails the check.

VERIFIED

Momus

A blind reviewer: control history and author notes are stripped first. It lists defects against the stated spec. Missing inputs return need_more, which is not approval.

VERIFIED

Hephaestus

Checks residual consistency, that no scars are left, and the budget guard. A supplied Hermes budget must stay at or under its max items. The check is not a Kin seat. Forge patronage is below.

VERIFIED

Nike

Passes or fails strictly against explicit success criteria. With no criteria, it fails.

VERIFIED

Atlas

Checks declared loads against the real capacity functions. An undeclared load is not invented. Visual patron only. No new capability claim.

Studio legions BETA

Plan, ingest, forge

Visitors learn and ask in chat. Choose a username there before a thread is saved. Signed-out use is a short per-network daily trial.

  • Plan / test / debug Hermes dispatches the loop. Mutating steps stay refused until Apollo proof. Lower-tier output stays out of the verified step.
  • Graphify Ingest through Eat: accept, reject, or abstain. Learn only on accept when commit is set.
  • Forge Hephaestus patronage, not a Kin. A video legion on Hermes. A configured provider can draft. Otherwise the status is UNAVAILABLE and no URL is invented. Generation from a prompt is in research, not shipped.
