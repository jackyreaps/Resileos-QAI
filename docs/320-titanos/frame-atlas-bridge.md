## 2. Corrected product stance

1. **Atlas Part 1** remains proposed/descriptive for *schools of thought*. It is not product truth.
2. **Dimensionality as participation ratio** is native-adjacent: it measures how many independent directions a matrix of cases×criteria (or steps×features) actually uses. That is legitimate **learning telemetry** for a gated residual machine.
3. **φ-compress and golden ladders are not optional metaphors.** They are implemented contraction and spectrum structure (RES-401, RES-607, ingest weights). Rank folds walk $\hat\varphi$; oscillators avoid rational resonance via φ-powers.
4. **Conscious-structure learning** (suite direction): improve residual use, gate honesty, and multi-axis coherence under contraction — not maximize open-ended generation. $D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and first-axis share are readouts of whether the system is collapsing onto one axis or holding several.

## 3. Where $D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ may attach (ordered)

**Phase A — offline script** (immediate, safe)

- Input: arrays from probes (coupled engines, path-B features, loop_history scars, band activation patterns).
- Output: `participation_ratio`, `first_axis_share`, optional level bin — same definitions as the Human-Medium dimensionality scale.
- Artifacts only; no change to `/answer`.

**Phase B — substrate monitor** (when Phase A is stable)

- Optional fields under `/stats` or substrate diagnostics, e.g. effective dimension of a rolling residual feature window.
- Never overrides truth-frame or invents rows.
- Gated by config flag (default off until validated).

**Phase C — learning signal** (research)

- Use drops in $D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ under load as a **collapse / over-constraint** warning (Eidolon-class *pattern*, not political content).
- Use φ-compress steps as intentional rank descent; compare $D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ before/after contraction under R-gate (RES-401 design intent).

Forbidden: scoring external ideological instruments online as if they were store facts.
